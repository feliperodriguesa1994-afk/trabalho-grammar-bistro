The tournament

Two teams at a time: Semifinal 1, Semifinal 2, third place and the Final. The winner of the duel goes on.

Tower and bell

The order is secret. Both teams take the cup tower down, build it again and run to the bell. The first team to ring it answers.

Levels and rebound

The levels come in the same order: 10 countable, 20 a/an/some/any, 30 build the sentence. In the last two orders the team that is losing chooses. There is no timer: the team can talk. The customer waits (green bar), then complains and the order loses points — never less than half. A wrong answer costs half the points. Then the other team can take the rebound: a new customer, same level, win or lose half.

Help, cards and guesses

A hint costs 5 points and the translation costs 10. Ask the class is free, once per duel. A problem card costs 15: you get a random problem and give it to any team. The teams outside guess (+5) if the team is going to be right or wrong.
